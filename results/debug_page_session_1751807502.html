Earlier Not Guilty Pleas

You entered an earlier plea of Not Guilty to the Traffic Violations Bureau (TVB) traffic tickets listed below. The TVB location and the date of the most recently scheduled TVB hearing are listed.
You can:
  • Change your plea to Guilty if you are eligible to change the plea, or
  • Check for other available hearing dates if you are eligible to change the date, or
  • Take no action now.
If you change your plea to Guilty, you agree to pay the fines, the fees and the surcharges required by law. The fines, the fees and the surcharges for the violations will be displayed only after you select and submit a plea of Guilty.
If you plead Guilty to an offense committed while operating a bicycle or in-line skates you are not required to pay a surcharge. Convictions for bicycle/in-line skate offenses do not result in points on your driving record.
If other hearing dates are available, this transaction will later display a page where you can select another available date. If you do not select a different date, the hearing date will remain the date displayed below.
Select the hearing location link to see the complete address of the hearing location.
If you select to Take No Action Now, you will not have answered the ticket or entered any plea.
If you fail to answer a traffic ticket, the DMV will impose additional fees, enter a default conviction, and suspend your driver license.
