Plead to or Pay Traffic Violations Bureau Traffic Tickets

Please Note:

  • To use this transaction, the front of your traffic ticket must display the words "Traffic Violations Bureau" at the bottom.
  • You cannot use this transaction to answer a traffic ticket that does not display the words "Traffic Violations Bureau" and that lists a local traffic court or a criminal court as the location to answer the traffic ticket.
  • Do not enter a driver license number from another state or nation. If you do not have a New York State driver license, permit or non-driver ID, return to the beginning of the transaction and answer No (My license, permit or non-driver ID is from another state.)
  • If you do not have the Traffic Ticket Number, enter the driver information, the last four digits of your Social Security Number and your email address.
  • Enter your last name and your first name as the names appear on your New York State driver license, permit, or non-driver ID. If you do not have a New York State driver license, permit or non-driver ID, return to the beginning of the transaction and answer No (My license, permit or non-driver ID is from another state.)
  • If your name contains a hyphen, enter the hyphen. Do not enter other punctuation.
  • If you have a middle initial on your New York State driver license, permit or non-driver ID, enter it. If you do not have a middle initial on your document, do not enter one.
  • If you have more than one TVB traffic ticket, you will see information about your other tickets.
  • If you have a question, read about how to plead or pay a New York State traffic ticket Opens new window.
  • If you need help understanding your ticket, please see sample traffic tickets Opens new window.
  • If you need help, please contact the Traffic Violations Bureau (TVB) at 718-488-5710.
